Community Guidelines

The short version of how we keep OverVibez safe and fun. The Terms are the full rules.

Adults only

OverVibez is for people aged 18 and over. Anything involving someone under 18 is banned, is reported to the authorities, and leads to a permanent ban.

Consent matters

Only post content of people who have agreed to it being shared. Never share private or intimate images of someone without their consent, and never post anything made, faked or altered to look like someone who did not agree to it.

Be decent

No harassment, threats, hate, doxxing or pile-ons. Disagreement is fine; targeting people is not. If someone bothers you, block or mute them from their profile.

No scams, spam or cheating

No fake accounts, spam, impersonation, misleading offers, or trying to get around payments or paid content. One person, one account. Duplicate accounts are removed and disqualified from giveaways.

Keep it legal

No illegal content, no content that promotes violence, exploitation or trafficking, and no content you don't have the rights to. Report anything illegal with the ⚑ button; reports like these are looked at first.

Paid content and money

Creators keep 85% of what they earn. Be honest about what you sell. Don't share paid content outside the platform.

If we take action

We may remove content, restrict features, or suspend accounts. We'll tell you why, and you can appeal from the notice or the suspension banner. A team member who wasn't involved in the first decision reviews it where possible.

Report a problem

Use ⚑ on any post, comment, message or profile, or the Help & contact link in the app.
