Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

1. Who is responsible

OverVibez (United Kingdom) is the controller of your data. Contact: use the Help & contact link in the app.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: email address, username, display name, account type, date of birth (for the 18+ check), password (stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider).
  • Content you create: posts, media, comments, messages, reports, profile details.
  • Activity: likes, follows, unlocks, subscriptions, tips, raffle entries, and your wallet and transaction history.
  • Verification data: we confirm you are 18 or over in one of two ways. By hand: a member of our team checks your passport or driving licence on a video call, or from a photo you email us, which we delete as soon as it has been checked. Through Stripe Identity (when we switch it on): Stripe processes your government ID and a selfie. Either way, we keep only the result (verified or not), the date, and a short note of what was checked — we do not keep copies of your ID document.
  • Payment data: card payments are handled by Stripe. We do not receive or store full card numbers.
  • Giveaway data: if you win a prize, the delivery name, address and phone number you give us, used only to send the prize.
  • Safety data: who you block or mute, reports you make, and appeals you send.
  • Technical data: basic logs such as IP address and device information for security and abuse prevention.

3. How we use it

To run the service, process payments and payouts, keep the platform safe (age checks, fraud and abuse prevention, moderation), comply with the law, and communicate with you about your account. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Who sees it

Your username, display name, bio, posts you make public and comments are visible to other users. Messages are visible only to the people in the conversation, and to our moderators if a message is reported. Paid content is shown only to people who have paid or subscribed. Wallet balances and earnings are private to you.

5. Service providers

We use Supabase (database, authentication and file storage, hosted in the UK, London), Vercel (website hosting), Stripe (payments, payouts and ID verification), LiveKit (live video streaming, EU region) and Resend (email). They process data on our behalf under their own terms.

6. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores a sign-in session and a few settings on your device (for example that you confirmed you are 18+, and which feed tab you last used). These are strictly necessary to run the service and cannot be turned off without breaking it.

7. Retention

We keep account data while your account is open. Financial records are kept as long as the law requires. Moderation records are kept as needed to enforce our rules and protect users. When you delete your account we delete or anonymise your personal data, except where we must keep it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict some processing, and to complain to your data-protection authority. Contact us through the Help & contact link in the app.

9. Security

Access to data is restricted by row-level security rules in our database, paid media is stored privately, and sensitive operations such as payments run on the server. No system is perfectly secure; tell us through the Help & contact link in the app if you find a problem.

10. Children

OverVibez is for adults only. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18 and will delete it if we find it.

11. Changes

We will update this policy as the service changes and tell you about material changes.
